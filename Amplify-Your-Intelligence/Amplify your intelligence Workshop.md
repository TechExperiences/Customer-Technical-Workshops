# 	Microsoft Technical Workshop: Build AI Context Across People, Business & Knowledge with Microsoft IQ

### Overview about Amplify Your Intelligence

**Workshop Purpose:** This workshop helps technical teams design and deliver integrated Microsoft IQ solutions that unify work context, business data, and enterprise knowledge into a single intelligence layer. 

In this workshop, we will use Caldova Pharmaceutical’s accelerated NextGen Pharma launch as our business scenario. Caldova needs to determine whether it can close a 7% production-capacity gap across three plants before the November launch. We will explore how Microsoft IQ brings together manufacturing data, GMP guidance, external market information, and organizational context to support that decision.
